# First Responder Standard Operating Procedure
**SOP ID:** SOP-OMEGA-IR-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Case:** OMEGA-2026-001

## Purpose
Secure and document a suspected digital incident scene while minimizing alteration or loss of potential evidence.

## Procedure
1. Receive the report and record the reporter, role, contact, exact time, location, and symptoms.
2. Confirm authority and scope with the incident coordinator.
3. Consider life safety and continuity of clinical services first. In a real hospital, coordinate with clinical leadership.
4. Establish scene access control and maintain an access log.
5. Observe before touching. Record device power state, logged-in session, displayed messages, attached media, cables, and visible network connections.
6. Photograph the overall room, each device, screen contents, ports, labels, and cabling. Assign photo IDs and preserve originals.
7. Create a scene sketch and network topology diagram. Mark device and evidence IDs.
8. Do not casually power off or reboot systems. Volatile memory and active connections may be lost. A trained examiner should decide whether live acquisition is necessary and document the method.
9. Coordinate any containment/isolation with the authorized incident lead. Record who approved it, what was isolated, when, how, and operational impact.
10. Identify potential evidence: endpoints, servers, removable media, network equipment, logs, cloud audit records, and backups.
11. Assign unique evidence IDs; record make/model, serial number where available, hostname, location, state, and condition.
12. Package and seal physical items appropriately. Restrict access and document every opening or transfer.
13. Begin the Chain of Custody form immediately.
14. Handover evidence and notes to the forensic examiner with a signed/acknowledged transfer record.
15. Complete contemporaneous notes. Correct mistakes transparently; do not erase or backdate entries.

## Scene documentation checklist
- [ ] Overview photographs
- [ ] Device and screen photographs
- [ ] Cable/port photographs
- [ ] Room sketch
- [ ] Network topology
- [ ] Evidence tags
- [ ] Access log
