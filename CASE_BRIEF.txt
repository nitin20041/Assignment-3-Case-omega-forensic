CASE OMEGA – CASE BRIEF

Case ID: OMEGA-2026-001
Organization: Omega Multispeciality Hospital (fictional)
Scenario date: 17 November 2026
Initial incident notification: 09:00 IST
Investigation type: Academic digital-forensics simulation

INCIDENT SUMMARY
Hospital IT staff reported that selected shared files appeared with a .locked extension 
and a ransom-style note was found. The scenario includes simulated email, endpoint, login,
 USB, firewall, file-audit and helpdesk events.

INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVES
1. Document the scene and first-responder actions.
2. Organize synthetic evidence and maintain a Chain of Custody.
3. Calculate SHA-256 hashes to check file integrity.
4. Demonstrate ransomware-like file transformation safely.
5. Correlate synthetic logs and build an event timeline.
6. Prepare a forensic report with findings and limitations.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
All organizations, people, records and logs are fictional/synthetic.
No real patient data or hospital systems are involved. 
The evidence ZIP is not a raw forensic disk image. FTK Imager and Autopsy
acquisition/examination have not been completed in this package.
Generated visuals are not screenshots from those tools.
